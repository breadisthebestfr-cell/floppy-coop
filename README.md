# Floppy Co-op (working title)

A silly online co-op game with floppy gummy movers. Carry furniture, deliver pizzas and fly a plane full of chaotic passengers, alone or with up to 4 friends.

**This is an early test version.** Things will be broken, ugly or unbalanced.

## Download + play (Windows)

1. Go to **[Releases](../../releases)** (on the right side of this page) and download the newest `FloppyCoop-....zip`.
2. Right-click the zip -> **Extract All**.
3. Double-click **FloppyCoop.exe**.
   - Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the game isn't signed. Click **More info** -> **Run anyway**.

## Play together (Steam)

Both players need **Steam open and logged in**, and you need to be **Steam friends**.

1. One player (the one with the stronger PC) clicks **Host Game**.
2. In the game, press **Esc** -> **Invite friends** (top right) -> **Invite** next to your friend's name.
3. The friend opens the game, then clicks the invite in their Steam chat. Or they click **Join a Friend** in the game's menu -> **Join**.

(Steam will show you as playing "Spacewar". That's Valve's test app, and it's normal for now.)

## Old / slow PC?

Press **Esc** in the game -> **Settings** -> **Graphics** -> **Quality: Potato (old PCs)**, then close the menu (the game restarts once).

## Controls

WASD walk, Space jump, Left/Right mouse = grab with the left/right hand, E use, F flop over, R respawn, Esc menu + settings.
